Splitwell – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Splitwell ("the app") is made by Wellbits Studio. It works without an account, and most of what you enter stays on your phone.

What stays on your device

Your settings, history and everything you type into the app are stored only on your phone. We do not have servers that receive them. Deleting the app, or tapping Delete all my data in Settings, removes them.

This includes your trips: the names of the people, expenses and amounts you enter never leave your phone and are not sent to us, to advertisers or to analytics. When you tap Share, the summary goes only to the app you choose.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

  • In the EU, UK and some US states we ask for your consent first, and you can change it anytime in Settings → Privacy options.
  • On iPhone, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking.
  • Splitwell Plus members do not see ads.

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

App analytics, crash reports and push notifications (Google Firebase)

  • Firebase Analytics records app usage events (for example which screens are opened) with an app instance ID, device model, OS version, language and approximate country. It does not record your name, email or what you enter in the app.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes or hits an error: device model, OS version, app version and the technical error.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your phone a push token so we can send occasional news. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings at any time.

Google processes this data on our behalf: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Notifications (optional)

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No reminder data leaves your phone.

Purchases

If you buy Splitwell Plus, the payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive your card details.

Children

Splitwell is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Turn reminders off in Settings at any time.
  • Tap Delete all my data in Settings to erase everything stored by the app on your phone.
